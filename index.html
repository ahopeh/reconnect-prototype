How it works

Back online in four steps

1

Request a phone

Tell us your phone, your carrier and how to reach you. It takes a couple of minutes.

2

Instant check

Answer a few questions and our form checks them right away, any hour. If everything checks out, you sign the agreement and place a refundable hold on your card. If we need to talk first, we'll call you during business hours.

3

Pick it up

Your locker code shows on screen and goes to your email. Scan your photo ID, then grab your phone any time, day or night. It works right away on a temporary eSIM while we restore your number.

4

Get back online

Follow the step-by-step guide for your phone and carrier. We check in the next business day to make sure it went well. Return it to any partner locker when you're done.
