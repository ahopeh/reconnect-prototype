    Good to know

    How the hold and card on file work

    Think of it like a rental car. Bring the phone back the way you got it, and your hold is released. We keep a credit card on file. The hold equals the phone's replacement price because that's what it would cost us to replace it quickly.

    We charge the full price of the phone if:

    • the phone isn't returned
    • it comes back locked because it was reported lost or stolen
    • it comes back too damaged to use

    If anything goes wrong, call us right away. The sooner we know, the more we can help. Full terms are in the loaner agreement.
