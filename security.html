Security and privacy

Your phone, your accounts, your control

You are handing your digital life to a loaner phone, so here is exactly how we protect it. The short version: we never need your passwords, and we keep very little.

What happens to every loaner

Before you get it

Enrolled and checked

The phone is registered to us, reset, and verified clean: no accounts, no activation lock, and an ID (IMEI) that has never been reported lost or stolen.

While you use it

Protected in the background

Threat protection watches for malware, harmful apps and phishing links. If the phone goes missing, we can locate and lock it.

After you return it

Erased and logged

We erase it remotely, confirm nothing of yours is left, and record the wipe before the next traveler gets it.

The software behind it

The same kind of tools IT teams use to manage company phones. Our team works with them every day.

Mosyle

Deeper control for our iPhone loaners
  • Locate, lock or erase a phone remotely
  • Stops anyone else from resetting or taking over the phone
  • Confirms each phone is clean before it goes out

NinjaOne

Manages our whole fleet, iPhone and Android
  • Remote locate, lock and erase on both platforms
  • Keeps every phone updated with security patches
  • One inventory of every loaner and where it is

SentinelOne

Protects every phone while you use it
  • Catches malware and harmful apps
  • Flags phishing links and unsafe Wi-Fi
  • Alerts us to a threat before it reaches your accounts

Our promises

Passwords

Your passwords stay yours

You type your own passwords, PINs and login codes. We never see them, store them, or ask for them by phone, text or email. Anyone who does is not us.

Carrier

We never see your carrier account

You move your own number with your own carrier. Our guides show you the steps. Your carrier decides how to verify you, and we do not bypass it.

Your data

Nothing of yours is kept

We do not copy or back up your photos, messages or files. Every phone is erased and verified after every rental.

How we check it is really you

Five checks before a locker opens

  1. Instant check. The request form confirms your phone, carrier and account are something we can help with before you pay.
  2. Texted code. We text a code to the phone number you give us, so we know you can be reached there.
  3. Signed agreement. You read and sign the loaner agreement, including the charges.
  4. Refundable card hold. A credit card in your name, matching the name you signed. Debit cards are not accepted for the hold.
  5. Single-use locker code. Your code arrives by text and opens one locker for your pickup. Take a quick photo of the phone when you open it, so we both know its condition.
Payments

How your card is handled

Card numbers stay with the processor

Your card goes through a secure payment page run by Stripe. We never see or store your full card number. The hold is released when you return the phone. See Services & Pricing for exactly when we would charge it.

Your privacy

What we keep, and what you get a copy of

What we collect

  • Your name and contact details
  • Where you are staying
  • Your payment details, held by the payment processor
  • The request answers and signed agreement

We do not sell your information.

Copies go to your email

Everything you fill out or sign is emailed to the address you list on the request form: your request, the signed agreement, your hold receipt and your locker code. Please double-check the spelling when you enter it.

If something goes wrong

If the loaner is lost, stolen or damaged, or you think someone has your locker code, call us right away at (808) 555-0142. The sooner we know, the more we can do. Full terms are in the loaner agreement.
