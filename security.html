Supporting page · Security and privacy

Your data stays yours

Every loaner is erased, checked, and locked down before it reaches you, and erased and checked again when it comes back. Here's exactly what we do.

ICON: shield and phone
Life of every loaner phone
1 Erase→2 Verify clean→3 Lock down→4 Hand off→5 You use it→6 Return→7 Erase and verify again
A

Before you get it

  • Full factory erase and fresh software install
  • Checked: no accounts signed in, no leftover data
  • Software updated to the latest version
  • No pre-installed apps beyond the basics
  • Checklist signed by our technician and logged by serial number and IMEI
B

On the phone (protection from malware)

  • Apps only from the official app store
  • Built-in security protections turned on
  • Installing apps from unknown sources blocked
  • Automatic security updates on
  • Screen lock required at setup, set by you
  • [Optional managed settings to limit risky installs, to evaluate]
C

While you have it

  • We never ask for your passwords
  • We never see or copy your photos, messages, or accounts
  • We don't back up your data
  • The device can be locked remotely if it's lost [location use only per agreement, to confirm]
  • Your carrier account stays between you and your carrier
D

After you return it

  • You sign out of your accounts, and we check
  • Full erase, then a verification checklist
  • SIM or eSIM removed
  • Chain-of-custody log: who had it, when, and what we checked
  • Not loaned again until the checklist is complete
If something goes wrong

Lost, stolen, or suspicious activity

Call us right away. We lock the phone, help you protect your accounts, and record what happened. [Response process and contact to confirm]

What we don't claim

No method is perfect, so we'll never promise zero risk. Treat sensitive accounts carefully on any shared device, and sign out when you're done.
