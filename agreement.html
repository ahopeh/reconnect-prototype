1. The loaner phone and who owns it

Reconnect Maui LLC (“Reconnect,” “we”) is lending you the phone and accessories listed on your Rental Summary above (the “Loaner Phone”). You are the person named in the Rental Summary (“you”).

We own the Loaner Phone the whole time. Borrowing it doesn't transfer ownership, and you may not sell, lend, rent, or pledge it. It may be new or refurbished. Its serial number and IMEI are on your Rental Summary.

2. Rental period and returning the phone

2.1 Return date. Please return the Loaner Phone and everything that came with it by the Return By date on your Rental Summary.

2.2 How to return it. You can return it to any partner locker or location, or schedule a pickup. A return counts once we confirm we have it.

2.3 Extending. Need more time? Contact us before the Return By date. If we can say yes, the new date becomes your Return By date and these terms still apply. Days beyond the Return By date are billed at the daily rate.

2.4 If it isn't returned. If we haven't heard from you and the phone hasn't come back 3 days after the Return By date, we'll treat it as not returned and Section 6.2 applies. We'd much rather talk first, so please call us.

3. Fees and payment

3.1 What you pay. The daily rate for each day you keep the phone, a one-time setup fee, and any optional support you choose (Section 11). Your Rental Summary and our Services & Pricing page list the amounts. Each calendar day, or part of a day, counts as one day.

3.2 Card on file. You authorize us to charge your card on file for the fees above, any late days, and any charges described in Section 6. We'll send a receipt by email.

3.3 Missed payments. If a payment doesn't go through, we'll let you know and ask you to update your card. We may apply your deposit to the unpaid amount (Section 4).

4. Refundable hold

4.1 What it is. We place a refundable deposit hold on your card for the amount on your Rental Summary. It depends on the phone model. It works like a rental car deposit: it protects against damage while the phone is with you, a missed payment, and similar costs that insurance would normally cover.

4.2 Getting it back. If the phone comes back as we gave it to you and you're paid up, we'll release the hold within 3 business days of inspecting it. Your bank may take extra time to show it. If we use part of the deposit, we'll tell you what for and release the rest.

5. Taking care of the phone

5.1 Care. You're responsible for the Loaner Phone from the moment we hand it over until we confirm it's back. Please take reasonable care of it: protect it from drops, water, sand, and heat, and don't leave it unattended or in a parked car.

5.2 Lost or stolen. If it's lost, stolen, or damaged, tell us right away at (808) 555-0142. The sooner we know, the more we can help. We may lock the phone, mark it as lost, or locate it using device-management tools. We use location only if the phone is reported lost or stolen, or hasn't been returned. If you find it again, let us know.

5.3 No repairs. Please don't try to repair the phone or have anyone else repair it. Contact us instead.

6. Condition on return and replacement charge

6.1 Return it as you got it. Please return the Loaner Phone and its accessories in the same condition we handed them over, apart from normal wear from ordinary use. We inspect each phone when it comes back.

6.2 Full replacement charge. We'll charge your card on file the full Replacement Price on your Rental Summary if: (a) the phone isn't returned; (b) it comes back locked because it was reported lost or stolen; or (c) it comes back too damaged to use. We apply your deposit first.

6.3 Repairable damage. If it comes back damaged but still repairable, we'll charge the reasonable cost to fix it, up to the Replacement Price. Missing accessories are charged at their listed price. We'll send you our inspection notes and photos.

6.4 If you disagree. Contact us within 14 days of a charge. We'll go over the inspection notes with you and correct any mistake.

7. Who can use it, and how

Only you may use the Loaner Phone. When you pick it up, you will show a government-issued photo ID that matches the name on your Rental Summary and the card used for the hold. We use it only to confirm who you are. Please use it lawfully and keep it in Hawaiʻi unless we agree otherwise. Don't jailbreak or root it, remove device-management settings, alter the hardware, or remove serial or IMEI labels.

8. Your phone number and carrier

Your carrier controls your phone number and your service. If you move your number to the Loaner Phone, you do that with your carrier, who will verify you. We never contact your carrier for you and never see your carrier account. Any carrier fees are yours. Every Loaner Phone comes with a temporary eSIM so it works as soon as you pick it up. The temporary line has its own number, and your own number takes over once your carrier moves it. It includes calls, texts and data for the length of your rental.

Before you return the phone, please move your number back to your own phone or to a new one. We remove any SIM or eSIM from the Loaner Phone at return.

9. Your accounts and data

9.1 Your passwords stay yours. You sign in to your own accounts. We never ask for your passwords and we don't copy or back up your photos, messages, or accounts.

9.2 Before you return the phone. Sign out of all accounts and remove any account locks (for example, Activation Lock on iPhone or Factory Reset Protection on Android). Back up anything you want to keep first. After you return it, we erase and verify the phone. If it still can't be erased because it's tied to your account, we'll ask you to remove it. If that isn't done within 3 days, it counts as returned locked under Section 6.2(b).

9.3 Data left behind. We aren't responsible for personal data left on the phone or erased from it.

10. Software

Software on the Loaner Phone is licensed to that device only. Please install apps only from the official app store, with valid licenses. Don't copy, reverse engineer, disassemble, or decompile any software, equipment, or components.

11. Support services

Your setup and the step-by-step activation and return guides for your phone and carrier are included. Optional extra help is available at the rates on our Services & Pricing page: a pre-recorded video library (a small fee), a video call billed per 30 minutes, or an in-person visit, which costs the most. You only pay for what you choose.

12. Privacy

We collect your name, contact details, where you're staying, and payment details so we can deliver the phone, support you, and process charges. We don't sell your information.

13. Limits on our responsibility

Reconnect isn't an emergency service. Please don't rely on the Loaner Phone as your only way to reach emergency help, and service depends on your carrier and local coverage. To the extent the law allows, we aren't liable for indirect or consequential losses, or for loss of data, arising from this agreement or your use of the Loaner Phone. Nothing here limits any right you have under law that can't be limited.

14. Ending early

We may ask you to return the Loaner Phone sooner than planned if it's being misused or there's a safety or legal concern. If so, we'll tell you why and give you a reasonable time to return it, usually 24 hours.

15. General terms

This agreement and your Rental Summary are our whole agreement. Changes must be in writing and agreed by both of us. If any part isn't enforceable, the rest still applies. Electronic signatures and records are valid and have the same effect as paper and ink, as the federal E-SIGN Act and Hawaiʻi's Uniform Electronic Transactions Act (Hawaiʻi Revised Statutes chapter 489E) provide. See Section 16. Hawaiʻi law governs this agreement, and any dispute will be handled in the courts of Maui County, Hawaiʻi.

16. Electronic records and signature consent

To sign online and receive your documents by email, we need your consent. When you sign, you confirm you have read this section.

  • What it covers. This rental and the records that go with it: this agreement, your Rental Summary, receipts, hold and charge notices, inspection notes and support messages. It doesn't cover other transactions.
  • You can get paper. You may ask for any record on paper, at no charge, by emailing hello@reconnectmaui.com or calling (808) 555-0142. You can also print or save this agreement now with the button at the top of this page.
  • You can withdraw consent. You can withdraw at any time, with no fee, by emailing or calling us. If you withdraw before pickup, we'll finish your rental by phone and paper instead, and it may take longer. Withdrawing doesn't affect anything you've already signed.
  • Keeping your email current. Tell us if your email address changes. We send everything to the email on your request.
  • What you need. A device with internet access, a current web browser, an email account you can open, and a way to open or print PDFs. Your phone, tablet or a computer all work.
  • Your signature. When you type your name and select Sign, you intend it as your legal signature, with the same effect as a handwritten one. We keep a record of who signed, when, and the exact version signed. You can get a copy of that record any time.
