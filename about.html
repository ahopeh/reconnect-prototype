About Us

Local students who got tired of watching visitors get stuck

When a visitor's phone fails on Maui, a store can be a long drive away and nobody lends them a phone. We started Reconnect to close that gap. We began as a UH Maui College capstone project and are building it with local hotels and shops.

Our mission: get travelers back online the same day.
