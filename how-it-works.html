Supporting page · What to expect

Getting your phone and number back, step by step

Clear steps, no surprises. You stay in control of your accounts, and we tell you up front what we can and can't do.

1

Tell us what happened

You

Call, text, or fill out the form. Share where you're staying, your phone model, and your carrier. A borrowed phone is fine.

2

We check what's possible

Reconnect

We tell you honestly whether your carrier and phone can be moved, and what you'll need. If it won't work, we say so before anything is delivered. We prepare a wiped, verified loaner.

3

Handoff and loaner agreement

ReconnectYou

We bring the phone to you, confirm you're the person who requested it, and you review and sign the loaner agreement.

4

Your carrier moves your number

YouYour carrier

You contact your own carrier to link your number to the loaner. They'll verify you, usually with your account PIN or passcode, and sometimes photo ID. We give you the loaner's IMEI and eSIM ID to share, and we sit with you while you do it.

We never contact your carrier for you or see your account.

5

You restore your accounts

YouReconnect guides

Sign in to your own Apple or Google account, restore your backup, and set up login codes. We walk you through it on screen. You type your own passwords, and we never ask for them.

6

Return it and move on

YouReconnect

Sign out of your accounts, move your number to your own or new phone, and return the loaner using the pickup or drop-off option we set up with you. We erase and verify it.

Have these ready

  • Account holder name and billing ZIP
  • Carrier account PIN or passcode
  • Photo ID
  • Access to your email
  • Apple or Google account sign-in
  • A backup way to get login codes

What's an IMEI?

A phone's ID number. Your carrier links your number to the phone you're using. We give you the loaner's number to share with them.

What we can't do

  • Switch a work-managed phone. Your employer's IT must approve it.
  • Skip your carrier's verification.
  • Recover accounts you can't sign in to.
