Good to know

How the deposit and card on file work

We want you to know exactly how this works before you say yes. Think of it like a rental car: bring the phone back the way you got it, and your deposit comes back to you. We keep a card on file when you request a phone.

We would charge the full price of the phone if:

  • the phone isn't returned
  • it comes back locked because it was reported lost or stolen
  • it comes back too damaged to use

If anything goes wrong, call us right away. The sooner we know, the more we can help. Full terms are in the loaner agreement. [Replacement price per model to confirm]
